FAQ

Common questions

How long until I see results?
SEO builds over time. Expect early signals in months 1-3, meaningful ranking movement in months 3-6, and consistent traffic growth from month 6 onwards. The businesses that see the best results are the ones that start earlier and stay consistent.
Do you guarantee rankings?
No honest SEO service can guarantee specific rankings — Google controls the results. What we can guarantee is proven, transparent practice designed to improve your visibility, plus complete clarity on what we're doing and why at every stage.
What's included in the free audit?
A full review of your website covering title tags, meta descriptions, blog content, keyword gaps, Google Business Profile, and technical issues — with specific, prioritised recommendations. No sales call required. The audit is genuinely free with no obligation.
Can I upgrade or change package?
Yes. You can move between packages at any monthly renewal point. Most clients start on Starter or Growth and upgrade once they see the early results.
Do you work with businesses outside the UK?
Yes. Wordwise works with local businesses across the UK and the US. International packages are priced in USD. Contact us directly for a proposal.
