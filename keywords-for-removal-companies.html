How to use these keywords

Three rules for removal company keyword targeting

01

One page per location

Google needs a dedicated page for each location you want to rank in. A single page mentioning five towns in a paragraph ranks for nothing specifically. Build one page per primary service area.

02

Start with low competition

If your site is new, start with the green keywords — cost guides, specialist services, moving guides. They rank faster, build authority, and feed traffic to your commercial pages over time.

03

Commercial is the priority

Office removals keywords have much lower competition than residential. One dedicated commercial page and two supporting articles targeting "office removals [your town]" can own that search in most markets.
