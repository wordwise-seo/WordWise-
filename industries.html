Why niche matters

Generic SEO advice doesn't work for local businesses

Every industry searches differently

A removal company customer searches "movers Daytona Beach." A yoga student searches "pregnancy yoga Surrey." A tradesperson's customer searches "emergency plumber Dorking." Generic content misses all three.

Industry knowledge changes the content

13 years in the removals industry means Wordwise understands the calendar of a moving company, the language of removal quotes, and what actually converts. That knowledge goes into every article.

Niche content ranks faster

A specific article targeting "caravan cleaning Buckinghamshire" has almost no competition. A generic article about "cleaning services" competes with every cleaning company in the country.

Specialist vs generalist

When a yoga studio owner receives a pitch from a general SEO agency and a specialist who has built an entire yoga marketing hub — they know which one understands their business.
