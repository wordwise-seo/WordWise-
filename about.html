Wordwise is an SEO and website service for local UK and US businesses — built by someone who spent over a decade in the removals industry before realising that the companies doing the best work were often the ones nobody could find online.

I spent 13 years working in the removals industry. In that time I worked with sole traders running one van and companies with full fleets covering the whole country. The same problem came up again and again: brilliant businesses — reliable, careful, honest — losing work to competitors who ranked better on Google.

It had nothing to do with service quality. It was content. The companies at the top of local search results were not the best movers. They were the ones who had been publishing locally targeted articles, building location pages, and keeping their Google Business Profiles active. Most of the best operators I worked with had no blog, one service page, and a Google profile last updated in 2019.

"The companies winning on Google weren't the best movers. They were the ones who'd started publishing content before everyone else."

Wordwise started from a simple observation: local businesses across the UK — not just removal companies — were invisible online despite doing genuinely good work. Tradespeople, yoga studios, cleaners, landscapers, care homes. The gap between their actual quality and their online visibility was the business opportunity.

The service is built around one principle: content that is useful, locally targeted, and honest about what it does. No jargon. No lock-in contracts. No generic blog posts written by someone who has never been near the industry.

Wordwise is based in Dorking, Surrey. We work with local businesses across the UK and with US-based clients — primarily in the removals and moving company sector, where the industry knowledge gives us a genuine edge over generic marketing agencies.
