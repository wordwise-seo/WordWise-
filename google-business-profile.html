Common mistakes

Why most Google Business Profiles underperform

After auditing Google Business Profiles across Surrey and beyond, these four issues come up on almost every small business profile.

Wrong primary category

The primary category tells Google what your business is. Choosing "General Contractor" instead of "Electrician" reduces visibility for the searches that matter most.

No photos uploaded

Profiles without photos receive 35% fewer clicks than those with photos. Most businesses either have no photos or only uploaded their logo and nothing else.

Inconsistent business name

If your business name on Google differs from your website — even slightly — it sends a weak local signal to Google and reduces map ranking.

No reviews strategy

Reviews are the most powerful local ranking signal available. Most businesses wait for reviews to arrive rather than actively and consistently asking satisfied customers.
