Questions

The bits people ask

Does it pretend to be me?

No. FirstReply sends a quick, honest holding reply in your voice that confirms the enquiry and keeps the customer warm. You still call back to win the job. It never tries to close the sale or impersonate you on a call.

Do you need access to my website?

No. For most setups you just forward your enquiry notification emails to me with one simple rule. No logins, no passwords, no changes to your site.

What if it gets a reply wrong?

I tune it with you at setup and again after go-live, so the wording sounds like you. The replies are deliberately simple and safe. Confirm, reassure, ask one question.

Do I own my leads?

Yes, always. Every lead is logged in a sheet that is yours. If you ever stop, you keep your leads and your inbox.

Which businesses is it for?

Any local business that takes enquiries and competes on response speed. Trades, removals, recovery, dental, salons, lettings and more.
