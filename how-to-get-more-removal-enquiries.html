Eight strategies

How to get more removal enquiries

Ranked by speed of results. The first three can generate quote requests within days. The last three build long-term results over months.

01

Complete your Google Business Profile

The map results that appear at the top of Google for "removal companies near me" come from Google Business Profiles. A complete profile with photos, services, hours, and regular posts can generate enquiries within days of being optimised.

Local SEO guide for removal companies →
02

Build dedicated location pages

One general page mentioning multiple areas ranks for nothing specific. A dedicated "house removals Shrewsbury" page ranks for Shrewsbury searches. Ten location pages is a fundamentally different business from one catchall service page.

Full SEO guide for removal companies →
03

Ask your best customers for Google reviews

Send five recent customers a direct link to leave a Google review. A personal message from the person who managed their move converts at a significantly higher rate than a generic request. Five genuine reviews improve your map ranking measurably.

Website tips for removal companies →
04

Publish a moving cost guide for your area

"How much do removal companies cost in [location]" is the highest-converting content search in the removals niche. Someone researching costs has a confirmed intention to move. Most local removal companies have no content targeting this search.

Removal company marketing guide →
05

Target commercial and office removals

Commercial moves generate the highest revenue per job in the industry. The SEO competition for "office removals [location]" is significantly lower than residential removal keywords — yet almost no removal companies have content specifically targeting it.

How to get commercial removal contracts →
06

Build estate agent and solicitor relationships

Estate agents, solicitors, and conveyancers all have clients who need removal companies. A referral arrangement with two or three local property professionals generates a consistent stream of warm leads — people who are already confirmed as moving.

Full marketing guide →
07

Publish consistent blog content

Moving checklists, packing guides, area guides, seasonal tips — each article targets a specific search and compounds over time. The removal companies that dominate local Google results are almost always the ones that started publishing content earliest.

Best keywords for removal companies →
08

Target specialist and piano moves

Piano moves, antique furniture, specialist items — these generate premium rates and have almost no local competition in Google. A single article targeting "piano movers [your town]" could rank in the top three within weeks and generate high-margin enquiries.
