Why Wordwise

Why wellness businesses need specialist marketing

Generic marketing advice does not account for how wellness clients actually search, decide, and book. Wordwise builds content around the specific patterns of the health and wellness market.

01

Niche keyword research

Wellness searches are specific. "Yoga for perimenopause Surrey" converts at a far higher rate than "yoga class near me." Understanding the difference between browsers and buyers is the foundation of effective wellness SEO.

02

Trust-first content

Wellness clients research carefully before booking. Content that genuinely informs — about a class type, a qualification, a condition — builds more trust than promotional copy. The audit page is always the goal, not the starting point.

03

Local authority

Most wellness businesses serve a specific area. Ranking locally requires specific signals — in title tags, in content, in Google Business Profiles. Wordwise builds those signals into every piece of content we produce.
