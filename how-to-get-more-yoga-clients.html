Eight strategies

How to get more yoga clients

These eight strategies are ranked roughly by speed of results. The first three can generate enquiries within days. The last three build long-term results over months.

01

Optimise your Google Business Profile

The fastest route to new local students. A complete, active Google Business Profile gets you into the map results that appear above organic search listings. Most yoga studios have incomplete profiles. Completing yours takes an afternoon and generates results within days.

Full Google Business Profile guide →
02

Target specialist class keywords

One article targeting "pregnancy yoga Guildford" or "yoga for perimenopause Surrey" can rank within weeks for a search with almost no competition. The person who finds that article is far more motivated to book than someone searching "yoga class near me."

Find the best yoga keywords →
03

Ask your best students for Google reviews

Send five of your most loyal students a direct link to leave a Google review. A personal message from a teacher they value converts far better than a generic newsletter ask. Five genuine reviews improve your map ranking measurably.

Local SEO and reviews guide →
04

Build partnerships with complementary businesses

Physiotherapists, osteopaths, nutritionists, personal trainers, and midwives all have clients who would benefit from yoga. A referral arrangement with two or three local complementary practitioners generates warm, high-quality leads consistently.

See all yoga marketing ideas →
05

Pursue corporate yoga contracts

Corporate yoga clients pay more per session, book in blocks, and provide guaranteed income. The pitch is straightforward: staff wellbeing, reduced sick days, improved focus. One corporate contract can be worth more per month than ten individual clients.

How to get corporate yoga clients →
06

Offer private and one-to-one sessions

Private yoga clients pay premium rates and often become long-term clients. They typically search specifically for private instruction rather than group classes. Creating content targeting "private yoga teacher [your location]" puts you in front of this high-value audience.

How to get private yoga clients →
07

Publish consistent blog content

One locally targeted blog article per month builds topical authority steadily. After twelve months, a studio with twelve locally specific articles consistently outranks competitors in local search results. The studios winning on Google are almost always the ones that started blogging first.

SEO for yoga studios guide →
08

Get online yoga clients

Live streaming and video libraries expand your potential audience beyond your local area. Clients in different cities or countries who cannot attend in person can still practice with you. Targeting "online yoga classes UK" or "live yoga streaming" opens a national audience.

Specialist audiences

Three high-value yoga client types

Beyond general class bookings, these three client types generate higher revenue per relationship and are actively searching for what yoga studios offer.

Corporate Clients

HR managers and wellbeing leads searching for employee yoga programmes. Block bookings, higher rates, long-term contracts. The pitch focuses on staff wellbeing and productivity rather than personal practice.

Target: "corporate yoga [your city]"

Private Clients

Individuals seeking one-to-one tuition for specific goals — injury recovery, a specific style, flexible scheduling. Premium rates, loyal long-term relationships, and strong word-of-mouth referrals.

Target: "private yoga teacher [your area]"

Teacher Training Applicants

People considering a significant personal and financial investment. They research carefully and extensively before applying. One well-ranked teacher training page can be worth more than any other single page on your site.

Target: "yoga teacher training [location]"
