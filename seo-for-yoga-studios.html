The problem

Why yoga studios stay invisible on Google

After auditing more than five yoga studios across Surrey, the same issues come up every time. None of them are about the quality of the classes.

01

No location in the page title. Someone searching “yoga studio Guildford” sees no location signal in your headline — and clicks a competitor instead.

02

Blog dormant for months or years. The most reliable way to rank for local searches — and the most consistently neglected tool on every yoga studio website.

03

No meta descriptions — or the same generic one on every page. Google writes its own version, and it almost never says what you need it to say.

04

Specialist classes invisible on Google. Pregnancy yoga, perimenopause yoga, hot yoga, reformer Pilates — all high-intent niche searches with almost no local competition.

These are fixable problems. Wordwise fixes them — with content that ranks and copy that converts.

What we look for

The yoga studio website audit

Every Wordwise audit for a yoga studio covers these seven areas. Most studios have issues in four or five of them — none of which are visible to visitors but all of which affect Google.

01

Title tags

Does your homepage title include your location and your primary service? “Yoga Studio in Guildford, Surrey” ranks. “Home | Studio Name” does not.

02

Meta descriptions

Is every page on your site accompanied by a compelling, locally targeted description? Missing or duplicate meta descriptions mean Google writes its own — and rarely gets it right.

03

Blog content and publishing frequency

Is your blog active? When was the last post published? A dormant blog sends a signal to Google that the site is not being maintained. An active blog with locally targeted content sends the opposite signal.

04

Specialist class targeting

Does each specialist class — pregnancy yoga, perimenopause, hot yoga, reformer Pilates, suspension yoga — have its own dedicated page or article? Niche searches convert at a higher rate and have far less competition.

05

Image alt texts

Does every image on your site have descriptive alt text? Alt texts are an underused SEO signal — and most yoga studio sites leave them completely blank.

06

Local signals

Does your site mention your town, postcode, and coverage area consistently? Local signals in page content, footer, and contact details strengthen your ranking for location-specific searches.

07

Technical errors

Broken links, placeholder text left from web design, outdated copyright dates, missing canonical tags — small technical issues that add up to a weaker overall SEO position.
