The channels

Six ways to market your yoga studio

Each channel works differently. Each has a different cost, time investment, and payoff. Here is an honest assessment of all six.

🔍

Search Engine Optimisation

Getting your studio to appear on Google when someone searches for yoga classes in your area. Slower to build than paid ads but the results last indefinitely and cost nothing per click. The highest-ROI channel for most studios over a 12-month horizon.

Read the full SEO guide →
📍

Google Business Profile

The map results that appear at the top of Google when someone searches "yoga studio near me." Your Google Business Profile determines whether you appear there. Free to set up. One of the fastest ways to generate local enquiries from people who are ready to book.

Read the local SEO guide →
📝

Content Marketing

Blog articles, guides, and resources that answer the questions your potential students are already searching for. Builds authority and trust before a single enquiry is made. The compound interest of yoga marketing — each piece published continues to generate results for years.

Read the keyword guide →
📱

Social Media

Instagram and Facebook are the most natural platforms for yoga studios. Strong for community building and retaining existing students. Less reliable for acquiring new students who have never heard of you. Best used alongside SEO rather than instead of it.

See yoga marketing ideas →
🎯

Paid Advertising

Google Ads and Facebook Ads can generate enquiries quickly but require ongoing spend to maintain results. Effective for specific campaigns such as teacher training intake periods or new studio launches. Expensive as a long-term primary acquisition channel for most studios.

Read the digital marketing guide →
🤝

Partnerships and Referrals

Relationships with complementary businesses — physiotherapists, nutritionists, personal trainers, GP surgeries — that refer clients to your studio. High quality leads because they arrive with a recommendation. Slow to build but generates some of the highest-converting enquiries.

Where to start

What to do first

If you are starting from zero or trying to prioritise limited time, this is the order that generates results fastest for most yoga studios.

01

Set up and complete your Google Business Profile

Free, fast, and generates map results immediately. Name, address, phone, categories, photos, services. This one action can generate enquiries within days. Most yoga studios have an incomplete profile or no profile at all.

02

Fix your homepage title tag

Add your location to your homepage title. "Yoga Studio in Guildford, Surrey | Studio Name" tells Google exactly who you are and where you are. A two-minute change that improves your local search visibility permanently.

03

Write one specialist class article

Pick your most distinctive class — pregnancy yoga, perimenopause yoga, hot yoga, reformer Pilates — and write a locally targeted article about it. Specialist keywords are far easier to rank for than generic "yoga studio near me" searches.

04

Ask your five most loyal students for a Google review

Send a direct message with a link to your Google Business Profile review page. Five genuine reviews from people who love your classes will improve your map ranking measurably within weeks.

05

Publish consistently — one article per month minimum

One article per month targeting a specific local keyword builds topical authority steadily over a 12-month period. The studios that dominate local Google results are almost always the ones that started publishing content earliest and kept at it.
