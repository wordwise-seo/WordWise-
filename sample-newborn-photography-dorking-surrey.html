Newborn Photography Sessions in Dorking, Surrey: What to Expect with Jessie Jane Photography

Target keyword: newborn photography sessions Dorking Surrey · ~900 words · © Wordwise 2026

The first days and weeks with a newborn pass faster than anyone warns you they will. The particular weight of a sleeping baby, the way tiny fingers curl, the expressions that flicker across a new face before they've learned to control them — these are the details that blur and soften in memory even as you're living them.

Newborn photography exists to hold those details still. And in Dorking, Surrey, Jessie Jane Photography offers newborn sessions that do exactly that — gently, naturally, and entirely at the pace of your family.

A Studio Session That Feels Nothing Like a Studio

Jessie works from a home studio in Dorking — a quiet, warm space with soft natural light and the kind of calm that a busy commercial studio rarely achieves. There is no conveyor-belt feel, no rushing to the next booking, no sense that your session is one of fifteen that week.

For a newborn baby — and for new parents navigating the exhaustion and wonder of the first weeks — the environment of a session matters as much as the skill of the photographer. A relaxed baby photographs beautifully. Jessie's studio in Dorking is built around this understanding.

The Best Time to Book a Newborn Session

Newborn photography sessions work best in the first two weeks of life — ideally between five and twelve days after birth. In these early days, babies sleep deeply and naturally curl into the peaceful, compact positions that make newborn images so distinctive.

Jessie recommends booking your newborn session before your due date — ideally in the second or third trimester. Many families book maternity and newborn sessions together at this stage.

What Happens During a Session

A newborn session with Jessie Jane Photography is unhurried and entirely led by the baby. There is no fixed timeline, no list of poses to work through. Sessions typically take two to three hours, though this varies significantly — some babies settle quickly, others need more time. Both are completely normal.

As a parent of two young children herself, Jessie brings genuine understanding of what those early weeks feel like — the tiredness, the tenderness, and the overwhelming love that makes these sessions both precious and sometimes delicate.

Beyond the Baby — Including Parents and Siblings

The best newborn images often include the people surrounding the baby as much as the baby alone. The way a parent holds their newborn, the expression on a sibling's face when they meet their new brother or sister for the first time — these are the images that people return to again and again.

Maternity and Cake Smash Photography in Dorking

Jessie also offers maternity photography sessions in Dorking and cake smash photography for first birthdays — creating a sequence of images that tells the complete story of a remarkable first year.
