What is SEO copywriting?

SEO copywriting is the practice of writing content that does two jobs at once. It reads naturally for the human visitor — clear, useful, and relevant to what they were searching for. And it's structured in a way that tells Google exactly what your business does and where it operates.

Here's a simple example. Someone in Dorking searches "how to move an upright piano." The first organic result is a piano shop in Cambridge. That Cambridge business is getting clicks from a Dorking search because they published a well-written article on exactly that question. The Dorking piano dealer who never blogged gets nothing — even though their shop is right here.

That's money on the table. And it happens in every local niche, every day, because most small businesses don't publish content and their competitors — sometimes businesses miles away — fill the gap.
