Yoga for Perimenopause in Surrey: Amy Miller's Midlife Classes in Dorking

Target keyword: yoga for perimenopause Surrey · ~900 words · © Wordwise 2026

Perimenopause does things to a woman's body that nobody adequately prepares you for. The broken sleep, the heat that arrives without warning, the brain that won't cooperate, the anxiety that seems to come from nowhere. And then — underneath all of that — the quieter question of who you are now, in this new territory.

Amy Miller's yoga classes in Dorking were built for exactly this moment. Not yoga as performance. Not yoga as fitness routine. Yoga as a way of coming back to yourself — through a body that feels unfamiliar, in a season of life that nobody talks about enough.

Why Yoga Helps with Perimenopause — and Why This Class Is Different

The evidence for yoga during perimenopause is substantial. Regular practice has been shown to reduce hot flush frequency and severity, improve sleep quality, reduce anxiety and low mood, and improve overall quality of life through hormonal transition.

But Amy's approach goes further than the physical benefits. The foundation of her teaching is cyclical living — the practice of aligning with the natural rhythms of seasons, moon cycles, and the body's own patterns. For women in perimenopause, whose hormonal rhythms are shifting significantly, this framework offers something a standard yoga class doesn't: a way of understanding what's happening rather than just managing the symptoms.

What Amy's Classes Offer Perimenopausal Women

  • Weekly yoga classes in Dorking — grounding, seasonal yoga for midlifers
  • Women's circles and moon gatherings — community, seasonal ritual, breath
  • Cacao ceremonies and Sabbat Soul Spa events — ritual for the inner landscape of midlife
  • Retreats in the UK and Spain — deeper immersion away from daily depletion
  • 1:1 mentoring — personalised support for the specific challenges of perimenopause

The Rock and Roll Edge

Amy describes her teaching as carrying "a rock and roll edge" — and her students recognise it immediately. One puts it simply: "She puts the cool into woo woo."

What this means in practice is that Amy's classes are accessible, warm, occasionally funny, and rooted in real life. The spiritual and seasonal elements are not decorative — they are practical tools for navigating a period of life that genuinely demands a different kind of support.

Based in Dorking, Surrey

Amy teaches in Dorking, Surrey — serving women across Dorking, Leatherhead, Reigate, Guildford, and the surrounding villages. She also offers online sessions for those who can't make it to the studio in person.
