FirstReply · Speed to Lead

Why replying first wins more customers.

By Ben Adamson·Wordwise·Dorking, Surrey
← FirstReply

When someone fills in your contact form, sends a message, or asks for a quote, they want help right now. The trouble is, most businesses reply too slowly, and a slow reply hands the job to whoever got back to them first.

Every missed lead is lost money

When a customer contacts you, their interest is at its absolute peak in the first few minutes. They have a problem they want solved and they are ready to talk. The longer you take to reply, the colder that interest gets, and the more time you give a competitor to win the conversation instead.

Most businesses still take hours to respond. Some take days. By then the customer has usually messaged two or three other firms and booked with the one that answered quickly and confidently. The work was there for the taking. It just went to someone faster.

5 minutes

Replying within the first five minutes dramatically increases your chance of winning the customer. Wait longer and that chance drops fast.

What today's customers actually expect

People have got used to instant everything. They expect a business to get back to them quickly, and when it does not, they do not wait around. They simply move on to the next name on the list.

Here is what loses them:

  • Going to voicemail and never getting a call back
  • A contact form that disappears into an inbox nobody checks until tomorrow
  • A delayed reply that arrives after they have already booked elsewhere
  • A cold, generic autoreply that says nothing useful

What they want is simple. They want to feel like a real person has seen their enquiry and is on it. The business that gives them that feeling first usually earns the conversation, and more often than not, the sale.

The hidden cost of slow follow-up

This is the part that stings. Most businesses spend real money getting enquiries in the first place, through Google, SEO, Facebook, and local directories. Then a slow reply quietly wastes that spend.

Every enquiry that goes unanswered for too long is a leak in your pipeline. It means lower conversion rates, missed bookings, lost trust, and a poorer return on every pound you spend on marketing. You paid to make the phone ring. Answering slowly is like letting it ring out.

How FirstReply fixes it

FirstReply is the speed-to-lead system I built to solve exactly this problem. The moment an enquiry lands, it sends a warm, natural reply in your voice, day or night, even when you are out on a job and cannot get to your phone.

It does not just fire off a generic autoresponder. It confirms the enquiry, reassures the customer that you will be in touch shortly, and asks one helpful question to move things forward. Then it grades the lead as hot, warm, or cold, and alerts you so you can call back while their interest is still high.

You still win the job the way you always have, by being good at what you do. FirstReply just makes sure you are the one who got there first.

Who it works best for

That includes removal firms, plumbers, electricians, roofers, recovery operators, cleaners, dental practices, salons, and lettings agents. If a faster reply would win you more work, it is built for you.
