Removal company website tips.

By Ben Adamson·13 years in the removals industry·Wordwise
← SEO for Removal Companies

After auditing removal company websites across the UK and US, the same issues come up every time. Most are simple fixes. All of them directly affect how many quote requests the site generates.

This article is part of the Wordwise removal company SEO hub — a complete collection of guides covering every aspect of SEO and marketing for removal businesses. Related articles are linked throughout.

The foundation first

Before any specific tactic, the basics matter most. Your Google Business Profile, your homepage title tag, and one piece of locally targeted content are the three things with the most impact in the shortest time. Read the complete SEO guide for removal companies for the full breakdown.

Location pages are the biggest lever

One page trying to serve every area you cover ranks for nothing specific. A dedicated page for each of your primary service areas — with the location in the title tag, in the heading, and throughout the copy — is the single most impactful SEO action for most removal companies. Read how to get more removal enquiries for the full strategy.

Content is the long game

Moving cost guides, packing tips, area guides, moving checklists — each one targets a specific search and compounds over time. The removal companies dominating local Google results started publishing content before everyone else. The best time to start is now.

Commercial is the biggest opportunity

Office removals and commercial relocations generate the highest revenue per job with lower SEO competition than residential moves. Read the guide on how to get commercial removal contracts for a full breakdown.
