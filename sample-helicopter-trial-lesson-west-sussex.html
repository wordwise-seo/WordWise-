Helicopter Trial Lesson in West Sussex: Fly with Shoreham Helicopters at Brighton City Airport

Target keyword: helicopter trial lesson West Sussex · ~900 words · © Wordwise 2026

A helicopter trial lesson is the most direct way to find out whether flight training is right for you — and whether Shoreham Helicopters is the right school. Forty-five minutes in the air above the Sussex coastline, with an instructor who hands you control of the aircraft within the first few minutes. No commitment. No prior experience required.

Shoreham Helicopters operates from Hanger 7 at Brighton City Airport, Shoreham-by-Sea — one of the most scenic trial lesson locations in the South East. To the south, the English Channel. To the north, the South Downs National Park. To the east, Brighton and the coastline stretching toward Eastbourne. It is, by any measure, an extraordinary place to take your first flight.

What Happens During a Trial Helicopter Lesson

The session begins on the ground. Before you get into the aircraft, your instructor will walk you through the basics — the controls, how a helicopter generates lift, and what to expect during the flight. This pre-flight briefing is deliberately informal — the aim is to make sure you feel confident and informed, not overwhelmed.

Once airborne, you'll experience what makes helicopter flight unlike anything else: the ability to hover, to move in any direction, to descend vertically, and to fly at low level over terrain that a fixed-wing aircraft could never access. Within the first few minutes, your instructor will invite you to take the controls. Under their supervision, you'll feel directly what helicopter flight requires — the light but precise inputs, the coordination between hands and feet, the unusual sensation of an aircraft that responds to every tiny movement.

Most first-time fliers describe the experience as simultaneously more intuitive and more demanding than they expected. Both of those things are true — and both are what make helicopter flying so compelling.

Who Is a Trial Lesson For?

  • Prospective student pilots — the most efficient way to assess whether PPLH training is the right next step
  • Aviation enthusiasts — anyone who has always wanted to fly
  • Gift recipients — a helicopter experience over the Sussex coast is one of the most memorable gifts in the South East
  • People considering a career change into aviation

Why Shoreham Helicopters

Shoreham Helicopters is one of the UK's leading helicopter training schools, operating from Brighton City Airport with a team of experienced instructors and a fleet maintained to Civil Aviation Authority standards. The location is easily accessible from across West Sussex, East Sussex, Surrey, and South London.

What Comes Next

A significant proportion of trial lesson students go on to begin PPLH training with Shoreham Helicopters. The PPLH — Private Pilot Licence (Helicopters) — is the foundation qualification for helicopter flight. If you're considering training, the trial lesson is the natural starting point — it lets you experience the aircraft, assess your aptitude, meet the instruction team, and make an informed decision about whether to proceed.
