What your free audit covers

Title tag and meta description

What we find

Real issues from real UK small business websites

Critical
Title tag is just the brand name — no location, no service
The most common issue in the pipeline. A yoga teacher in Ashtead whose title tag reads "Emily In Yoga" — with no Ashtead, no Surrey, no yoga class mentioned — gets zero search traffic for anyone who doesn't already know her name.
Critical
Primary call-to-action button links back to the same page
Found on multiple sites: the BOOK NOW button on the homepage links back to the homepage. Visitors who want to book are sent in a circle and frequently leave. A 30-second fix that directly increases conversions.
Warning
Meta description is 216 characters — the most important line is never seen
Google displays around 155 characters. Sites with descriptions that run to 200+ characters have their location and service information cut off before it appears — the exact information that makes someone click.
Warning
Grammar errors on the homepage
"HELICOPER TRAINING", "cooperate clients" (instead of corporate), "holoidays", "lets" (missing apostrophe). Each one is a small but unnecessary credibility concern — visible to every visitor before they've decided whether to trust the business.
Warning
Copyright 5–9 years out of date
Footer copyright from 2017, 2019, or 2020 signals to potential customers that the site hasn't been maintained. A one-field update in any CMS — but it's the kind of detail that careful customers notice.
Opportunity
No locally targeted content — searches sitting empty
"Yoga classes Ashtead Surrey", "helicopter trial lesson West Sussex", "newborn photographer Dorking" — real searches with real local intent and almost no competing content. The businesses that publish first own these searches.
